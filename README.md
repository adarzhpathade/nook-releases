# Nook

An animated island at the top of your Windows 11 screen. Glanceable live activities, system controls, and status alerts in one place that stays out of your way until you need it.

## What is Nook?

Nook brings an animated, floating island to the top centre of Windows 11. It reacts to what you are doing, provides instant glances for live activities, and lets you manage everyday actions without breaking your workflow.

Powered by **Nook Flow**, Nook's motion engine delivers smooth, physics-based springs and transitions tuned for your display's refresh rate.

## Features

- **Live music:** Album artwork, animated equaliser waves, track change peeks, and playback controls.
- **Timer & stopwatch:** Independent countdown timer and precision stopwatch, running in the island or side-by-side.
- **Calls:** Live call duration with active voice indicator and one-tap mute.
- **Mic and camera privacy indicator:** Clear, visible status whenever an application accesses your microphone or camera.
- **Downloads:** Progress ring and percentage for ongoing downloads, with quick access when finished.
- **Volume, brightness, and battery pop-ups:** Clean, responsive pop-ups when adjusting levels or checking battery status.
- **Quick notes:** Glanceable pill notifications for system states such as Caps Lock, Num Lock, and network connectivity.
- **Cards scrolled with the wheel:** Scroll through activity and information cards directly from the island using the mouse wheel.
- **Smart, Fixed, and Hidden visibility:** Choose how the island behaves:
  - *Smart:* Visible on the desktop, gracefully hides over application windows until you reach the top edge (keeping active tasks visible).
  - *Fixed:* Pill remains visible at all times.
  - *Hidden:* Stays tucked away until revealed at the top edge.
- **Fullscreen handling:** Automatically hides during fullscreen apps and games, with an optional exception to keep calls and mic/camera indicators visible.
- **Weather by city:** Current weather for your chosen city.
- **Settings app with live preview:** A dedicated settings window with real-time preview of island styles, motion feel, and visibility options.
